Privacy Policy

RaceSim Fuel Calculator Discord bot · Last updated: 11 August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the RaceSim Fuel Calculator Discord application (“the Bot”) processes when you use it.

1. Who we are

RaceSim Fuel Calculator is a Discord bot that estimates race fuel for Assetto Corsa Competizione (ACC) sessions. It is operated by the Bot’s developer team. For privacy questions, contact us through the official RaceSim Fuel Calculator support Discord server.

2. Information we process

When you use the Bot, we may process:

  • Discord user ID and display/username
  • Discord server (guild) ID and server name
  • Channel ID where the command was used
  • Values you submit in the /fuel form (race duration, lap time, fuel per lap)
  • Basic technical logs needed to run and debug the Bot (for example timestamps and errors)

The Bot does not require Discord Message Content Intent and does not read your general chat messages. It only receives data from slash commands, modal submissions, and standard Discord interaction metadata.

3. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • Provide fuel calculations and respond to your commands
  • Operate, maintain, and troubleshoot the Bot
  • Monitor usage and prevent abuse
  • Improve reliability and features

We do not sell your personal data.

4. Storage and retention

Usage information may appear in temporary server/console logs on our hosting provider while the Bot is running. We retain logs only as long as reasonably needed for operation, security, and troubleshooting, then discard or overwrite them through normal log rotation.

5. Sharing

We may share data only with:

  • Discord — as required to provide the Bot through Discord’s platform
  • Hosting providers — infrastructure used to run the Bot
  • Authorities — if required by law

6. Your choices

You can stop using the Bot at any time. Server admins can remove the Bot from a server. Because Discord accounts are controlled by Discord, requests about your Discord account data should also be directed to Discord’s own privacy channels where applicable.

If you want us to review or delete Bot-related logs we control, contact us via the support server and include your Discord user ID.

7. International users

The Bot may be hosted and accessed from different countries. By using the Bot, you understand that information may be processed in the regions where Discord and our hosting providers operate.

8. Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country to use Discord). Do not use the Bot if you are not allowed to use Discord.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Bot after updates means you accept the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy: contact the team through the official RaceSim Fuel Calculator support Discord server.
